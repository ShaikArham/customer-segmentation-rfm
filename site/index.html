TRANSPARENCY

How this analysis works

01 / Define eligible orders

One row represents one order. Cancelled and fully refunded orders are excluded from RFM. Partial refunds reduce monetary value. The observation window excludes its end date.

02 / Score consistently

Recency is days since the last eligible order. Frequency counts distinct orders. Monetary is total net sales in integer cents. Quintile thresholds score each measure from 1 to 5; lower recency earns a higher score.

Ties stay together. Uneven segment sizes are expected. Thresholds remain fixed when you filter.

View scoring thresholds
03 / Test, don’t assume
Segment rules are transparent heuristics. Campaign ideas are hypotheses, not measured business outcomes. This project contains no predictive model, causal claims or lifetime-value estimate.
Customer-country attribution must be consistent. Money is EUR only. Returns use the order’s date; this is a net-order analysis, not a cash-flow ledger.
